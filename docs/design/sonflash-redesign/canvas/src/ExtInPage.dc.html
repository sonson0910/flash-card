The flood that rewrote a city’s plans overnight

When the river crested at dawn, residents who had spent years asking for new barriers found themselves on the front page. By noon, neighbourhood groups had demanded an immediate response from the council, and the mayor cancelled every other meeting on her calendar.

Engineers had warned for a decade that the embankments were temporary at best. What had been a slow argument about budgets became, in a single morning, a question of how fast a city can change its mind.
