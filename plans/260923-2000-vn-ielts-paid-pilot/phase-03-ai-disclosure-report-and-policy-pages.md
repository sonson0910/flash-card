# Phase 03: AI disclosure, report a wrong card, policy pages

Effort: 1.25d (red team #12: rules `hasOnly` update, immutability clause and rules tests for
`origin`). Blocked by: none (reports are operational records keyed by uid, not analytics).
Release: R1 (G1); the report dialog and the terms page may follow on 10-02 under the W0 cut line
(plan.md).

## Context

- GPT §E19 and P0 #6: disclose AI-generated content and give learners a way to report errors; sell
  to adults 18+; publish what data is processed and by which providers before selling.
- Cards generated by `generateCard` (`src/features/intake/cardIntakePipeline.ts:476-560`) carry no
  marker that they came from AI. The card face is rendered in `src/components/Flashcard.tsx`.
- Card updates by clients are validated by `isValidCardUpdate` (`firestore.rules:640-662` and the
  key checks that follow), which lists every allowed card key. A new card field that is missing
  from that list makes **every** client update of such a card fail (bookmark, edit), so the rules
  change must ship in the same gate as the server change (red team #12).
- The only public policy page is `public/browser-extension-privacy.html`. There are no terms of sale.
- Providers in use: Google Gemini (`functions/src/index.ts:95`), Pexels, Unsplash, Wikipedia
  (`:486-510`), dictionaryapi.dev and browser speech (`src/lib/audio.ts`), Google Translate from the
  extension (extension README), Firebase (Google Cloud, `asia-southeast1`).

## Requirements

1. **Disclosure:** a small, theme-aware line "Nội dung do AI tạo, có thể sai. Hãy kiểm tra nghĩa."
   on the back of AI-generated cards and in the capture result. Detect AI cards **only** by a new
   optional card field `origin: 'ai'` set at generation. There is no heuristic fallback: the earlier
   rule "AI-only fields present" also matched manual cards (red team #12). Cards created before the
   pilot show no line; pilot learners start with new cards that carry `origin`. Does not alter any
   existing effect.
2. **Rules for `origin` (red team #12):** `firestore.rules` accepts `origin` (`'ai'` or `'manual'`)
   on create and lists it among the allowed keys in `isValidCardUpdate`; an update may keep it but
   never change or remove it (`!changed.hasAny(['origin'])`). Rules tests prove a client can
   bookmark and edit an AI card and cannot change `origin`. The rules deploy precedes functions and
   hosting in the same gate.
3. **Report a wrong card:** a "Báo thẻ sai" action on the card back opens a lazy dialog with reason
   codes (`wrong-meaning`, `wrong-example`, `wrong-pronunciation`, `wrong-image`, `offensive`,
   `other`) and an optional note up to 280 characters. It calls a new callable `reportCard`
   (`{ cardId, reason, note? }`), which copies the card's generated fields from Firestore server-side
   into `card_reports/{autoId}` with `uid`, `reason`, `note`, `createdAt`, `status: 'open'`. Rate
   limit 30/hour/owner. The dialog tells the learner the card content is sent to the founder for
   review.
4. **Policy pages** (static, Vietnamese first, English summary): `public/privacy.html` (data held,
   providers and regions, analytics without sentences or URLs, retention, export and deletion,
   contact), `public/terms.html` (18+; Pro in the pilot is granted by the operator and no payment is
   taken in the app; the future price is the final amount including VAT once the accountant
   confirms it; no auto-renewal; AI accuracy disclaimer; no guarantee of exam scores). Link both
   from the sign-in surface, the account menu and the upgrade dialog. Keep the extension privacy
   page and link it. The privacy page is required before the first real learner (G1); the terms
   page before the upgrade dialog (G2).
5. Sign-in and first-run copy states the service is for learners aged 18 and over (no age gate
   beyond this statement in the pilot).
6. **Owner, 2026-09-24:**
   - **Shared-deck origin.** Cards imported from a shared deck are AI content: set `origin: 'ai'` on
     import.
   - **Report retention.** `card_reports` are kept 180 days: `reportCard` writes
     `expiresAt = createdAt + 180 days`, with a TTL at G1 (phase 11).
   - **Report action.** "Báo thẻ sai" appears on the back face in every card layout, including Zen
     mode, and is visible without opening a collapsed panel.
   - **Operator view.** The runbook gives a console query or small operator script to list and
     close open reports.

- Playwright: open an AI card, report it, see the confirmation; the card effects spec
  (`e2e/flashcard-remediation.spec.ts`) still passes.
- Bundle budget: the dialog is a lazy chunk; the initial bundle grows by the disclosure string only;
  CSS stays inside its budget (plan.md risks).

## Risk

| Risk | L × I | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| `origin` missing from the rules key list blocks all updates of AI cards | H × H if missed | Rules change and rules tests in the same gate; rules deploy first (red team #12) |
| Adding `origin` breaks other strict card schemas | M × M | Tests across client normalization, callable parser, rules and device mirror before G1 |
| Legal wording is incomplete | M × M | Pages state facts only; the accountant question on tax and invoices stays open |
| Report spam | L × L | Auth + rate limit; notes capped at 280 characters |

## Rollback

Hosting rollback removes the UI; the callable can stay deployed. Rules and the create parser keep
accepting `origin` after any rollback, because cards that already carry it must stay editable; roll
back the rules only to a version that lists `origin`. Older clients and documents without the field
keep working.
